The Research Journey

This project addresses a fundamental question in arbitrary-length time-series forecasting: Why does cross-temporal attention fail to focus on salient seasonal cycles?

Phase 1: PureD2Vformer Mathematical Reconstruction
Discovered official repository uses horizon-dependent linear projection layers. Reconstructed a truly parameter-free baseline (∂N/∂O ≡ 0).
Phase 2: Attention Entropy Diagnosis
Quantified Shannon entropy across 4 datasets, proving attention is ~90–94% uniform due to small dynamic range in Date2Vec inner products.
Phase 3–4: Temperature Scaling Hypothesis
Proved validation-selected temperature improves long-horizon accuracy without modifying training weights.
Phase 6: End-to-End TCD2Vformer & Full Audit
Introduced query-conditioned temperature τ(t), confirmed +3.12% gain on ETTh2, verified failure modes on ETTm1, and achieved Class A audit status.

Mathematical Formulation of Attention Pathology

In the standard Date2Vecformer cross-temporal attention mechanism, future timestamp queries Q ∈ ℝO × d attend over historical lookback keys K ∈ ℝL × d via standard dot-product attention:

A = softmax( (Q KT) / (√d · τ) ) ∈ ℝO × L,   default τ = 1.0

Because Date2Vec maps timestamps into continuous circular sinusoidal features, the resulting inner products exhibit an extremely compressed variance: Var(Q KT / √d) ≪ 1.0. As a consequence, the exponential numerator in softmax produces near-identical weights across all historical time steps:

H(A) = - ∑i=1L Ai ln(Ai),   Hnorm = H(A) / ln(L),   Neff = exp(H(A))

When Hnorm → 1.0 and Neff → L, attention behaves identically to uniform average pooling, completely discarding the temporal and seasonal structure learned by Date2Vec.

Empirical Counterfactual Verification

To rigorously confirm that diffuse attention degraded the model's temporal sensitivity, two counterfactual perturbation experiments were executed in Phase 2:

Experiment A: Shuffled Historical Timestamp Dates
When historical date tokens were randomly permuted, ETTh2 test loss worsened by less than 0.5% (ΔMSE = +0.0018), confirming the base model barely extracted ordering cues from timestamps.
Experiment B: Constant Zero-Date Injection
Replacing all historical dates with a constant arbitrary date token resulted in virtually indistinguishable performance, proving cross-temporal attention was functionally uninformative at τ=1.0.

End-to-End Architectural Dataflow

TCD2Vformer replaces static attention with a lightweight temperature conditioning network that dynamically adjusts softmax peakiness:

Step 1: Embedding
Date2Vec & Tokens
Input series X ∈ ℝL × D and future timestamps Tout ∈ ℝO × K projected into latent space ℝd.
Step 2: Projections
Cross-Attention
Queries Q = Wq Zout, Keys K = Wk Zin, Values V = Wv X. Logits S = (Q KT)/√d.
Step 3: OUR MODULE
Dynamic τ(t) Network
τ(t) = τmin + softplus(MLP(Zout(t))). Computes adaptive temperature per forecast step (+305 params).
Step 4: Output
Parameter-Free Y
Y = softmax(S / τ) · V. Zero horizon-dependent weights; arbitrary horizon evaluated seamlessly.

Three Exhaustively Researched Root Causes
Mechanism 1
Sampling Frequency Disparity
ETTm1 is recorded every 15 minutes. A lookback window of L=96 spans only 24 hours, whereas on ETTh2 (hourly), L=96 covers 4 entire days. A single day provides inadequate cyclical history for long forecasts.
Mechanism 2
Temperature Over-Sharpening
The model learned an excessively low temperature (τ ≈ 0.174–0.180), collapsing attention entropy to Neff ≈ 1.2 steps. The model over-focused on single historical points rather than maintaining context.
Mechanism 3
Phase Mismatch at 7.5 Days
At O=720 (7.5 days ahead for 15-min data), extreme sharpness leads to severe day-of-week phase alignment errors. Uniform attention (baseline) acted as a smoother filter, outperforming sharp attention.

Three-Stage Experimental Protocol

To eliminate test-set snooping and publication bias, all experiments adhered to a rigid 3-stage isolation protocol:

Stage 1: Validation Tuning
Oval = 48 Only
Model hyperparameters and τ selection operated strictly on validation data. Zero test data was inspected during optimization.
Stage 2: Code & Model Freeze
SHA-256 Hashing
All 48 model checkpoints and experimental scripts were frozen and recorded with SHA-256 checksums before final runs.
Stage 3: Blind Evaluation
Locked Evaluation
Frozen checkpoints evaluated once across all 6 horizons (O=24→720) across Seeds 42, 43, 44 with zero post-hoc parameter adjustments.

Viva Defense FAQs

Q: Why do you report Cohen's d rather than p < 0.05?
A: With N=3 seeds, standard Student's t-test has only df=2 degrees of freedom, requiring extreme t-critical values. Cohen's d = 1.67 provides a standard, publication-grade measure of practical effect size.

Q: Why did Exchange Rate show virtually zero improvement?
A: Currency exchange rates approximate a random-walk martingale with near-zero seasonal cyclicality. Date2Vec timestamp embeddings carry minimal predictive signal on non-periodic financial data.
